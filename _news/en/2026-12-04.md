On June 12th and 13th, the second edition of G3XPo will take place—an event that brings together developers, researchers, students, and the game development community at Unesp in Rio Claro!

The event will feature talks by game developers and researchers from various cities across Brazil, sharing different stories, addressing diverse trends, and bringing knowledge from many areas of game development, such as programming, design, art, music, and more.

Follow the news here on the website and also on the pages of our partner G3X, the game studies group at Unesp Rio Claro: [G3X](https://linktr.ee/g3xunesp)

The event will be free and will be streamed live!

# About Re-G3X

**Re-G3X (Research in Games, Gamification, Serious Games, and eXtended Reality)** is an inter-university research group formed through a partnership between ICMC-USP and IGCE-UNESP, along with collaborators from other institutions.

## Our Mission

We research and develop solutions in the areas of **Games, Gamification, Serious Games, and Extended Reality (XR)** , applying knowledge in:

- **Procedural Content Generation**
- **Intelligent Agents**
- **Software Engineering**
- **Human-Computer Interaction**

## Research Highlights

Despite being recent, the group has already published in high-impact journals and conferences, such as:

- *Expert Systems with Applications* (Qualis A1)
- *Applied Soft Computing* (Qualis A1)
- *IEEE Transactions on Games* (Qualis A3)
- *Interacting with Computers* (Qualis A2)
- *IEEE Conference on Games*, *CHI*, *SBGames*, among others.

### Featured Projects

- **Overlord System**: orchestrator of multiple procedurally generated contents, adaptable to different player profiles.
- **RUFUS Platform**: development of therapeutic and empathetic games.
- **COMBO Framework**: support for creating constructivist educational games.

## Infrastructure and Collaboration

We operate in an integrated manner using online tools such as:

- **Discord** – communication and project organization
- **Google Drive** – documents and meeting recordings
- **GitHub** – open source code and institutional website

Biweekly meetings follow a format inspired by Scrum's *Daily Meeting*, promoting transparency and agility in development.
